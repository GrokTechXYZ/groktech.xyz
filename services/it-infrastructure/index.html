01 / The foundation

Every layer
has a purpose.

A new environment, an upgrade or a recurring fault. I start with what the system needs to support.

Hardware procurement and server installation

I specify and source hardware, mount and cable servers, and configure storage and virtualization. Capacity, dependencies and how the equipment will be maintained all belong in the same conversation.

Business networking and failover

Switches, routing, VLANs, firewalls and VPNs. I design the network around how it will be used, configure redundant connections and test what happens when a link goes down.

Backups, monitoring and IT operations

Monitoring, patching, backups and recovery are part of the system. I trace recurring faults and work through how failures will be detected and how recovery will be checked.

Cloud and infrastructure planning

On-premises first, with cloud services when they are the right tool. I consider costs, dependencies and operational control alongside the technical requirements.

02 / Define the work

A scope you
can work with.

The work can cover an existing environment, a new installation or a specific problem alongside your IT team. Before implementation, we work through the current setup, the intended changes and what depends on them.

  • What must remain available during the work.
  • Hardware, access and network dependencies.
  • How to check normal operation and failure behavior.
  • Configuration notes and handoff requirements.

Tell me what is in place, what needs to change and any constraints on timing. You do not need a complete technical brief to begin.
