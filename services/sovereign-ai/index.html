08 / Before we talk

A few
answers.

Do I have to take a monthly support plan?

No. Setup and training are a standalone project. For an on-premises installation, you can take over ongoing maintenance after handover. If I host your equipment, the agreed hosting and infrastructure maintenance fees still apply.

What does a system cost?

The Launch starts at $12,000, Scale at $28,000 and Sovereign at $50,000, with hardware quoted separately at your approval. Care plans run $750, $1,500 and $2,975 a month. Your exact price is fixed in writing before any work begins.

Does training mean training a new AI model?

The included training teaches you and your team to use the system. Any custom model fine-tuning or larger document-processing project is a separate scope to discuss.

Will everything work without an internet connection?

That depends on the agreed setup. On-premises models can be configured for local use, while remote access to a hosted system needs connectivity. Downloads, updates and any external integrations have their own network requirements, which we review during setup.
